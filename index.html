THE ORIGINS

WHERE CULTURE MEETS COSMOS

I’m Darko Reese—a Las Vegas artist with a habit of collecting creative obsessions and turning them into something tangible. Music, poetry, visual art, fashion... apparently, I’ve never been very good at picking just one.

Lunar Lynx Custom Lab is where all those worlds get to play together.

✦

What started with custom clothing and handmade pieces is growing into a home for wearable art, rotating collections, and whatever strange little idea refuses to leave me alone. One day it might be rhinestones and Vegas glamour; the next, something nostalgic, a little unhinged, or entirely unexpected.

✦

Forever inspired by the raw beauty of this ugly world, and the incredible interpretations by my fellow artists.

At its heart, Lunar Lynx is about freedom of expression, storytelling through aesthetic, and community through creativity.

✦

I’m building this lab one experiment at a time, and you’re invited to see what happens.
